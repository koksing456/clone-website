## Copy audit

Above-the-fold navigation, status, headline, supporting copy and both calls to
action match the approved concept. No official affiliation, ticket-sale claim,
member-count claim, or copied Formula 1 copy appears.

## Intentional deviations

- The implementation uses original generated imagery instead of reference-site
  media.
- The brand mark is code-native `SEPANG WEEKEND`, not an imitation logo.
- The join button stays disabled until a real WhatsApp invite URL is configured.
- Repeated concept footer links were reduced to the pages and disclosures that
  currently exist.

No material visual mismatch remains after desktop and mobile inspection.
